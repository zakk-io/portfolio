Kinyarwanda Sign Language Recognition

Read the blog
Problem
Around 70,000 people in Rwanda rely on Kinyarwanda Sign Language, yet there is no public dataset, model, or tool that turns KSL signs into spoken Kinyarwanda.
Solution
An edge-first pipeline that classifies signs from hand geometry alone: MediaPipe hand landmarks feed a 1,180-parameter network, quantized to a 6.9 KB TensorFlow Lite model (~98.5% validation accuracy on 10 signs, 36k self-collected samples), with Kinyarwanda speech output.
MediaPipeTensorFlow LiteKerasOpenCVTinyMLEdge deployment
